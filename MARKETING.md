# Chaser

**Get paid faster. Stop chasing invoices manually.**

Chaser is the accounts-receivable tool that turns overdue invoices into predictable cash flow.  
See what’s late, send the right chase at the right time, and connect your accounting system in one click.

---

## The problem

Late invoices kill cash flow.  
Most teams still:

- Manually check who owes what  
- Copy-paste reminder emails  
- Lose track of which follow-up was last sent  
- Have no idea which customers will actually pay  

Every day of delay costs money. Every forgotten follow-up costs more.

---

## The solution

Chaser gives you a single Board that shows:

- Total amount overdue  
- Exactly how many days late each invoice is  
- The next recommended chase (friendly nudge → firm reminder → final notice)  
- One-click “Send Chase” that actually goes out  

Add invoices in seconds. Mark them paid when the money lands. Snooze when you need breathing room.

---

## Key features

| Feature | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Live Board** | Real-time view of every open invoice sorted by urgency |
| **Smart sequences** | Pre-built chase types with subject lines and copy |
| **One-click chase** | Send the next follow-up without leaving the board |
| **Accounting sync** | Pull open invoices from your books |
| **Live FX rates** | See multi-currency values instantly |
| **Auth Lab** | Production-grade OAuth 2.0 + PKCE, JWT, HMAC, and mTLS demos for secure integrations |

---

## Built for real integrations

Chaser is designed to connect to the systems you already use:

- **Xero** — OAuth 2.0 + PKCE  
- **QuickBooks Online** — OAuth 2.0 + PKCE  
- **Stripe** — Webhooks with HMAC verification  
- Any modern API using JWT bearer tokens or mutual TLS  

The included Auth Lab lets you explore and test every major authentication method used by serious financial software in 2026.
